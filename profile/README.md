## Blitz Trading

[Blitz](https://www.blitz-trading.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org_profile&utm_campaign=gh_org_profile&utm_content=intro) is a free DCA ladder trading bot that runs on your own exchange account. No subscription. Your funds stay on your exchange, and Blitz never asks for withdrawal access.

Supported: Bybit · Gate · KuCoin · Bitget · BingX · Hyperliquid (Hyperliquid adds a Blitz builder fee)
